Top HUD update
- Time, Moves, Crystals, and Coins now use four equal-height/equal-weight cards.
- Crystal and Coin values are no longer grouped into one heavier wallet card.
- All four cards use the same baseline, corner radius, typography, padding, and spacing.
- Each card gets a small vibrant accent line/border appropriate to its content.
- Crystal and Coin cards remain tappable for their information dialogs.
