# Group reflow + Home actions

- Dragged merged blocks remain rigid.
- Destination blockers are reflowed into the cells released by the dragged block.
- A fully obstructing merged group is moved as one rigid shape when its shape fits the released footprint.
- Otherwise only the obstructing members are assigned to the nearest released cells, minimizing visual travel.
- Home always exposes both Continue and New Game. Continue is disabled when there is no saved session.
- Milestone grid presets remain portrait/rectangular: 4x6, 5x7, 6x9, 7x9, 7x8.
